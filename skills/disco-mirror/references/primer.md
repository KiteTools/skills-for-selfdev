# A council of 24 voices

Imagine that your attention has a council rather than a single narrator. One member notices a contradiction; another notices that somebody has gone quiet; a third wants to finish the task before dinner. None sees everything. This exercise gives those functions names borrowed from Disco Elysium and describes them in original everyday language.

The four groups organize attention. A louder voice is not automatically wiser, and a quiet one is not a character flaw. Every useful function has a cost when it dominates the council.

## Intellect — making things intelligible

| Voice | What it contributes | What happens when it dominates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Logic | Tests whether a conclusion follows from its premises | A tidy argument can crowd out messy evidence |
| Encyclopedia | Retrieves facts and relevant background | Information grows faster than the decision |
| Rhetoric | Finds the structure of an argument and a persuasive response | Winning the exchange replaces understanding |
| Drama | Notices performance, roles, and presentation | Ambiguity starts to look like deception |
| Conceptualization | Connects ideas and invents a useful frame | Elegant novelty displaces the actual problem |
| Visual Calculus | Reconstructs spatial relations and sequences | A plausible reconstruction feels more certain than it is |

## Psyche — relating, imagining, and holding a direction

| Voice | What it contributes | What happens when it dominates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Volition | Keeps a chosen direction under pressure | Persistence becomes refusal to reconsider |
| Inland Empire | Offers images, intuition, and symbolic associations | An image is mistaken for evidence |
| Empathy | Notices another person's possible experience | Inferred feelings replace the person's own account |
| Authority | States boundaries and takes responsibility | Disagreement becomes a contest for rank |
| Esprit de Corps | Reads group roles, loyalty, and shared purpose | Belonging makes outside evidence harder to hear |
| Suggestion | Finds a socially inviting way to approach someone | Influence becomes more important than mutual choice |

## Physique — energy, sensation, and bodily urgency

| Voice | What it contributes | What happens when it dominates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Endurance | Carries sustained effort | Warning signals arrive only after depletion |
| Pain Threshold | Helps continue through discomfort | Avoidable pain becomes something to prove |
| Physical Instrument | Turns intention into direct bodily action | Force becomes the default solution |
| Electrochemistry | Notices pleasure, appetite, and reward | Immediate stimulation takes over the agenda |
| Shivers | Registers atmosphere and a broad sense of place | A mood becomes a story about how the world works |
| Half Light | Detects urgency and possible threat | Unclear situations are treated as attacks |

## Motorics — noticing and acting in the moment

| Voice | What it contributes | What happens when it dominates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hand/Eye Coordination | Supports precise movement and timing | Technical execution outruns consideration of consequences |
| Perception | Picks up small sensory details | Many signals compete without a useful priority |
| Reaction Speed | Responds and connects information quickly | The answer arrives before the question is understood |
| Savoir Faire | Finds a fluid, adaptable way through a situation | Looking effortless matters more than being clear |
| Interfacing | Understands tools through hands-on contact | Tinkering takes over from the original purpose |
| Composure | Maintains outward steadiness and reads presentation | A convincing exterior conceals a need for support |

First you choose the council you identify with or want to develop. Then we compare that picture with particular situations. A mismatch is useful information, not a failed answer. The result is a revisable story about where your attention goes, what it makes possible, and which signals could arrive earlier.

This is an independently created reflection exercise inspired by a game. It is not a validated personality assessment. Playing the game is optional.
