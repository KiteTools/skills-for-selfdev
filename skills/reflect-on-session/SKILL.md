# Reflect on a session

Respond in the user's language. This is a participant's processing workflow for their own consultation. It is not an evaluation of the consultant and does not conduct psychogenetic expertise or establish a cause on its own.

## Inputs and scope

Read the full supplied transcript and identify client and consultant from evidence. If roles are unclear, preserve the uncertainty rather than assigning claims to the wrong person. Retain timestamps or stable paragraph references. Read existing summaries or personal context only when included in the request or explicitly designated as working context.

The default deliverable is a session reflection. Update accumulated records and produce visuals only when requested. A request for a “full package with pictures” includes the visual workflow below. Do not upload session text to another service, synchronize a CRM, message anyone, or overwrite source transcripts merely to complete a summary.

## Process the session

1. Extract the client's request, observed unwanted results, consequences, and context. Attribute client reports as reports, not independently verified events.
2. Separate understandings the client stated from formulations inferred through the consultant's corrections. A correction does not prove that the client held the opposite belief. Label this second category “consultant's proposed interpretation.”
3. Extract both propositions and mechanisms: how something works, conditions, distinctions, criteria, and principles carried by examples. Aim for at least as much attention to mechanisms as to propositions, without inventing missing material. In an IMT session, “false” and “new/true understanding” are attributed method terms, not factual verdicts issued by the assistant.
4. Connect each proposed new understanding to the old interpretation it addresses. Record whether the client explicitly accepted, questioned, rejected, or did not respond to it. List unresolved interpretations as backlog.
5. Build one or two central practice chains: **situation → response → old understanding → proposed new understanding → client's choice → corresponding action → evidence to watch**. Two distinct session lines may need separate chains. Unknown links remain unknown.
6. Draft session-specific first-person reminders, morning and evening questions, and select useful exact consultant quotes. Read [the output template](references/output-template.md) for the full shape and counts. A first-person draft must never be presented as an accepted belief.
7. Extract evidenced strengths, recurring patterns, motivations, stable context, goals, and reported family facts. Keep a one-off event distinct from a recurring pattern. Do not infer hereditary causation or relatives' motives from a family anecdote.
8. Separately inspect the first third of the transcript for **reported prior applications**: the client describes an action or changed response that already occurred before this consultation, with a direct quote. An insight during the session, a plan, or a compliment from the consultant is not a prior success. State if none are found.
9. Recheck every substantial consultant passage for omitted contrasts, examples, model changes, conditions, and mechanisms. Every important new understanding should be covered by a reminder or explicitly excluded because it was rejected or remains unresolved.

## Keep choice and causality intact

In IMT work, recording an insight is not completion of retraining. Preserve the sequence discussed in the session: investigate the operating understanding and its origin → understand a proposed more accurate alternative → the client chooses → connect that understanding to corresponding action → hold and repeat them together → observe whether an enduring result develops. Behavior alone can be a temporary workaround; agreement alone is not enduring change.

The assistant organizes this record. It cannot supply missing expertise, choose the client's worldview, impose a spiritual goal, declare a family cause established, or report success without an actual result. Preserve the user's stated beliefs without converting them into universal factual claims.

## Optional accumulation and visuals

For authorized context updates, follow [accumulated context](references/accumulated-context.md). Preserve earlier dated evidence, source links, corrections, and uncertainty; do not flatten them into an increasingly confident biography.

For pictures, follow [visual reminders](references/visual-reminders.md). This supports three distinct posters and the original five interpretation diagrams plus two practice diagrams where the source supports that many. State shortages instead of inventing beliefs or episodes. Keep private identifying anchors out of prompts sent to external image services unless the user has explicitly authorized that disclosure.

Before delivery verify quotation accuracy, speaker attribution, reference links, adoption status, and every success claim. For generated files, read them back; for images, inspect their render. Report unavailable optional output honestly.
