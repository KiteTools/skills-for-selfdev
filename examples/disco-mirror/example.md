# A prediction corrected

**English** · [Русский](example.ru.md)

**Fictional example, written for this repository. No personal interview data.**

The following is an excerpt, not a complete interview or finished portrait.

**Assistant's tentative prediction:** “When a colleague challenges your reasoning, you tend to answer immediately and keep discussing until the disagreement is resolved.”

**Participant:** “Usually the opposite. Last week I asked for an hour, checked the spreadsheet, and came back with two errors in my own calculation. What bothers me is being pushed to answer before I have checked.”

**Appropriate next turn:** “The immediate-response prediction does not fit that example. When you take that hour, is the main impulse to verify the information, work out how to present your answer, or something else you would describe differently?”

**Why it matters:** The desired build is a starting point. A concrete contradiction changes the working model; it is not explained away as the participant's hidden personality.
