# Fictional workshop transcript

[P1] INSTRUCTOR: A practice record can be small. The purpose of the record we are discussing today is to help you decide what to try next, not to measure your value as a person. For this exercise, write the situation, what you tried, and what happened.

[P2] INSTRUCTOR: Imagine that you planned to sketch after lunch. On Tuesday you opened the notebook and drew for ten minutes. On Wednesday you spent that time looking for a pencil. Those are two different events. Writing "I am inconsistent" hides the difference.

[P3] LEARNER: So should I keep the pencil beside the notebook?

[P4] INSTRUCTOR: That is one possible next experiment. Put it there for the next three sessions and record whether getting started becomes easier. Three sessions will not establish a general rule. They can show whether the setup is worth continuing for you.

[P5] INSTRUCTOR: If you did not record a day, mark it unknown. Do not reconstruct the day from your opinion of how the week went. At the next review, ask whether your record gives you enough information to choose a next experiment.
