# Disco Mirror

![Abstract visual for reflection and an inner council of voices](../../assets/reflect.png)

**Meet the voices that shape your choices—and test the story against your actual behavior.**

Disco Mirror is a conversational selfdev skill inspired by the 24 skill voices of *Disco Elysium*. You choose the kind of inner council you identify with, then explore everyday situations that confirm or challenge that picture. You can use it without having played the game.

## What you get

A narrative portrait of your strengths, recurring reactions, and the costs of overusing a strength. The portrait distinguishes what your examples support from what remains a hypothesis. If you want, continue with one or two small balancing experiments or ask to see the provisional game-style build.

The useful moment is often a correction: “That is not what I do.” The interview should change its interpretation when your example contradicts it.

## Try it

```text
Use $disco-mirror. Walk me through the voices, then ask one question at a time.
I want to understand what happens when I receive critical feedback.
Keep the working profile in this conversation.
```

**Input:** your answers and specific episodes. **Output:** a calibrated written portrait; optional experiments. Allow time for an introduction, a desired build, and about six to ten calibration rounds. You may stop or skip the introduction.

## Requirements and privacy

An assistant that can read this skill and sustain a conversation. No API, game account, or paid external service is required by the skill. Your assistant provider's access and retention policies still apply. The skill does not create a profile file or write memory unless you ask.

This is a reflective metaphor, not a validated personality test or diagnosis. All explanatory wording is original. Game names identify the inspiration; no game artwork or dialogue is bundled. The project is not affiliated with or endorsed by the game's creators or rights holders.

**Release status:** portable instruction package. It preserves the source workflow's interview phases and correction rules; the public package has not been validated as a personality instrument or tested across all assistant hosts.
