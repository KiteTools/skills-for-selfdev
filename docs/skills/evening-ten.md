# Evening Ten

![Abstract visual for daily rhythm and small possibilities](../../assets/rhythm.png)

**Ten useful possibilities you might have missed in today's work and life.**

You edited a draft, had a conversation, tried something, or left a question unresolved. Evening Ten looks across those threads and finds small, concrete opportunities: a different format, a simpler next experiment, a connection between available resources, or a step that can be removed.

This is the portable version of the “Ten” in an evening assistant workflow. It gives you possibilities without turning your evening into another performance review.

## What makes a Ten

The assistant maps the supplied day's evidence, drafts twenty candidates, checks recent suggestions, and chooses ten. The selection should span different parts of the day and at least four kinds of move. At least three items explore a fresh direction rather than merely copying things you liked before.

Each item explains a concrete opportunity in about 220 characters. You can ignore all ten. Choosing one records interest, not completion—and the skill does not execute it for you.

## Try it

```text
Use $evening-ten. Here are my day notes, current goals, and the last week's lists.
Find ten concrete possibilities grounded in this material.
Cover the whole supplied day, check for repeated ideas, and distinguish observations
from suggestions. Do not create tasks or send messages.
```

**Input:** day summaries or a diary, optional stated goals, and up to fourteen days of earlier lists and feedback. **Output:** a short day summary, optional goal alignment, and ten numbered possibilities. A structured evidence and selection record is available when requested.

If the evidence cannot support ten useful ideas, the skill should say so and label a partial result. It should never manufacture an event to fill the list.

## Requirements and privacy

A plain diary works; Computer History is optional. The skill consumes summaries you supply or designate. It does not install an activity recorder or browse raw screenshots, private chats, or unrelated files.

**Release status:** portable on-demand instruction package. The source workflow delivered its report at 22:00, but this package does not install a schedule, collect activity, or connect to a messaging service. Those integrations need separate setup, a timezone, and user authorization. Repetition checks require earlier lists; without them the skill declares that limitation.
