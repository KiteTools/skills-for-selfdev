# Weekly Review

[English](weekly-review.md) · [Русский](weekly-review.ru.md)

![A week of observations brought together for reflection](../../assets/rhythm.png)

**See how learning became understanding, a choice, and something you actually tried.**

A busy week leaves many traces but no single reliable story. This skill brings supplied notes, decisions, and results together, preserves the gaps, and helps you choose one small experiment for the following week. It works in an ordinary conversation; a running assistant or activity tracker is not required.

## What it does

- Reconstructs the week from the material you provide, with source references and coverage limits.
- Separates activity, reviewed work, a decision, an attempted action, and an observed result.
- Uses five optional lenses: meeting a source, working toward understanding, applying to yourself, working with people who requested it, and applying in life situations.
- Separates live interaction from preparation and administration; overlapping activities do not create extra hours.
- Asks only about specific missing intervals that matter, after using the notes already available.
- Produces a short overview and a detailed review with one proposed experiment. Your goals and personal lenses remain yours to choose.

The five-lens approach comes from a personal IMT learning-review workflow. Here it is generalized: no inherited timetable, spiritual score, predefined teacher hierarchy, or assumed daily practice duration. A decision from GoR can enter the review as a decision; it becomes evidence of application only after an actual attempt is reported or observed.

## Input and output

**Bring:** the week's dates, timezone if time matters, notes or selected summaries, any explicit corrections, and goals only if you want a comparison. Optional evidence can include work artifacts and confirmed outcomes. A diary alone is enough.

**Receive:** a readable overview, daily evidence map, source → understanding → application episodes, confidence limits, and one optional next experiment. A visualization can accompany a deep review when the assistant supports it; a table works without extra tools.

## Try it

```text
Use $weekly-review for these notes from my week.
Keep plans, choices, actions, and results separate. Use the five lenses,
but do not infer understanding from time at a screen.
Ask only about gaps that would change the review.
```

[See a fictional week and its review](../../examples/weekly-review/README.md).

## Requirements and status

**Available:** portable instructions for an AI assistant, on demand. No OpenClaw, Telegram, Python, calendar connection, or Computer History installation is required. Reading an optional connected source still requires your authorization and the relevant tools. Saving a report or scheduling future reviews is a separate action.

The skill cannot recover an unrecorded week, establish an inner state from app use, or prove causality from a pattern. Duration conventions must be supplied by you and labeled as estimates. It does not automatically close goals or turn its experiment into a task.
