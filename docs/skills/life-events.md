# Life events: turn memories into a timeline

**English** · [Русский](life-events.ru.md)

![Illustration of the explore workflow](../../assets/explore.png)

Start with a few notes, a conversation transcript, or one event at a time. Get a clear chronology that keeps what happened separate from how you experienced it.

**Try it:**

> Use life-events to turn these notes into a timeline. Keep approximate dates, link every event to its source, and leave my emotional assessment blank when I haven't stated it.

## What you receive

- A readable table of events with dates or ages, reported impact, sources and review status.
- Duplicates combined without losing their sources; contradictory versions left visible.
- A short list of useful gaps, with optional JSON for your own private records.

For example, “I moved around age 24; it was exciting and I missed my friends” becomes an approximate event with both reported feelings. The assistant does not decide that the move was good or bad for you.

## Two ways to begin

**Manual capture:** describe an episode and answer one follow-up at a time. **Transcript extraction:** supply a selected transcript and review the proposed events before using them elsewhere.

## Requirements and status

Works with a text-capable AI agent that can read the [skill](../../skills/life-events/SKILL.md). No account or external service is required for a text timeline. A model provider may retain your conversation according to its own settings.

This is a new portable companion inspired by **SOOV / СООВ**, the earlier manual/transcript event-entry workflow. The original app is not bundled, and this file is not a claim of migration or import compatibility. See the [SOOV note](../integrations/soov.md).

## Keep the next step intentional

Save the timeline privately. You can use it to prepare a consultation or continue with [Longevity intake](longevity-intake.md). Longevity has its own schema and conditional model; a life-events timeline does not calculate longevity or infer causes.
